Our Story

Ten years in the making — the moments that brought us here, with a few snapshots from our adventures along the way.

  1. 2008

    Matt first laid eyes on Jacqueline in Princes Risborough Costa. Sadly, she was seeing a certain Jack Dobson at the time — better known to us all as the Best Man.

  2. 2014

    Jacqueline saw Matt stacking shelves in M&S and immediately knew he was the one.

  3. 2017

    We properly met at Hayley Beale's house party and started dating on the 6th May.

  4. 2017 Paris
  5. 2018 Thailand
  6. 2019 Iceland
  7. 2023

    We flew the nest and moved into our first flat together.

  8. 2023 Cambodia
  9. 2024

  10. 2025

    We bought our first home together on 1st August.

  11. 2025

  12. 2027

    We officially tie the knot on 6th May — our 10-year anniversary. Then on the 19th May we will welcome you to Hadsham Farm, sharing our vows and celebrating the start of our next chapter, surrounded by our favourite people.
