Dress Code

Pop on your glad rags and help us fill the tipi with colour! Our bridesmaids will be in soft pastels, so bright and colourful outfits are very much encouraged.

Just one gentle request — please avoid white and ivory.

We'll be celebrating in a tipi out in the countryside, so flat or block-heel shoes fare better on grass than stilettos, and we'd suggest a layer for the evening once the sun goes down.
